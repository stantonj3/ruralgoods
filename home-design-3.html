Our story

Rural Goods started as a family project focused on gentle Kunekune care. We believe small herds, clean housing, and everyday attention build healthier piglets and more confident owners.

"We raise our piglets the way we want our own animals raised: outdoors when weather allows, handled daily, and moved into homes only when they are ready."

What matters most

Pasture access, strong nutrition, and friendly socialization—these are the values that shape every litter we offer.
