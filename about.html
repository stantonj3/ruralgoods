About Rural Goods

Rural Goods is a family farm raising Kunekune pigs with care, respect, and sustainable practices.

Our story

At Rural Goods, we believe well-cared-for animals create a better experience for buyers and for the farm. Our Kunekune breeding program is built on pasture-based care, regular health checks, and socialization so that our piglets are calm and confident.

We raise our animals in a low-stress environment with thoughtful feed, clean water, and plenty of outdoor access. Our goal is to deliver strong, healthy piglets to families and small farms looking for an excellent companion animal.

Our values

  • Animal welfare first
  • Transparent breeding practices
  • High standards for health and hygiene
  • Support for new owners

Why Kunekune?

Friendly temperament

Kunekune are social, gentle, and easy to train, making them ideal for hobby farms.

Compact size

Smaller than many pig breeds, Kunekune adapt well to smaller paddocks and family settings.

Beautiful appearance

Their round shape, short legs, and sweet faces make Kunekune instantly charming.
